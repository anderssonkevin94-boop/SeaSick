# Deck visibility alternatives

Built-in image generation edits of the broad workboat player-view concept. Approximate design studies, not verified Unity renders; camera and geometry can drift. Options: open deck, below-deck cabin, side helm. Recommendation: broad open deck with small port aft helm perch; retain large stern wheel, low cargo and slender centered chimney. No gameplay assets or camera settings changed.
