## Correction

The bottom-right orthographic top view was rebuilt to match the established stern design in every other view.

- The deck terminates in two broad stern shoulders around a central U-shaped notch.
- The wheel cassette is recessed within that notch, behind and below deck level.
- The teal gunwale continues into a narrow raised bridge over the wheel.
- Blue-background gaps and visible mechanical depth prevent the bridge from reading as a solid roof.
- The transverse axle, hubs, socket rails, module seam, and locking points remain visible from above.
- The stern wheel is no longer represented as a barrel, box, or platform placed on the deck.

All other panels and established V3 modularity rules remain unchanged.
