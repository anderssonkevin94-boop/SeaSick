# Stern Paddle Astra Concept V3

Generated with the built-in image generation tool as a precise-object edit of V2.

## Design Goal

Simplify the stern silhouette and establish a reusable wheel-upgrade system that can survive changes in hull size, materials, and ornament across the player fleet.

## Permanent Hull Parts

- One continuous teal gunwale rising over the wheel in a smooth shallow arch.
- A broad symmetrical U-shaped opening in the stern.
- Two dark-steel vertical socket rails integrated into the hull cheeks.
- Two large attachment bolts per rail and generous clearance for larger wheels.

These parts belong to the ship hull and remain consistent between wheel upgrades.

## Interchangeable Wheel Cassette

- Paddle wheel, paddles, axle, hubs, minimal splash guard, and perimeter frame form one removable assembly.
- A narrow seam separates the cassette from the permanent hull socket.
- Two large upper locking pins communicate how the module is retained and released.
- The complete cassette slides rearward from the standardized mounting bay.

Wheel upgrades may change diameter, width, paddle shape, hub construction, materials, reinforcement, and ornament while preserving the socket location and attachment envelope.

## Visual Rules

- Read order: hull silhouette, raised teal rail, circular wheel, steel mounting rails.
- Prefer few large shapes, continuous lines, sparse oversized fasteners, and broad toon-shader-friendly facets.
- Avoid layered roofs, peaks, triangular decoration, dense bracing, and numerous competing seams.
- Keep the wheel's lower arc open to the water and its mechanical silhouette visible.
