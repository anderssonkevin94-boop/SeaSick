# Approximate player camera concepts

Built-in image generation edits of stern-paddle-concepts-v3. These are illustrative camera studies, not Unity screenshots or exact projection/occlusion verification. Sea.unity serialized camera: distance 20.8, height 6.4, lookAhead 14.4, lookHeight 1.2, framingMargin 1.25, fovBase 58; framing scales with hull. Cruise adds distance and height. Generated views may vary from those settings and from reference geometry.

## 01-open-launch

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an approximate in-game PLAYER CAMERA VIEW of the exact ship in the reference. Preserve ship design and proportions, huge center stern paddle, raised rear cabin and helm on its roof, tall midships centerline chimney, guns, cargo, small sailors and simple flat low-poly wooden/teal/steel game art. CHANGE ONLY VIEWPOINT and place ship sailing on a simple stylized blue ocean. Landscape 16:9 game screen. Camera is DIRECTLY BEHIND ship, precisely on longitudinal centerline, zero sideways angle, looking forward toward distant horizon. Low slightly elevated third-person chase view, NOT overhead, NOT isometric, NOT three-quarter side view. Camera station approximately 26m aft of ship center and 8m above sea for a 24m ship, scaled with hull size; 58-degree vertical perspective FOV, shallow downwards optical tilt around 9 degrees looking ahead. Ship occupies lower central portion, enough sea around it and sky above, horizon near upper third. Closest feature is broad face of huge stern paddle drum: from exactly astern see horizontal wooden paddle blades spanning left-right, circular side rims foreshortened, NOT a side-on circular wheel. Lower paddle blades submerged and simple white churn/wake trails toward viewer. See rear cabin windows above/behind paddle, back of helmsman on quarterdeck, chimney aligned centrally further ahead. Physically truthful occlusion: the raised stern cabin and giant paddle obscure much of the lower forward main deck, only visible portions of cannons and crew at sides and beyond cabin should show. Do NOT lift camera to expose whole deck; do NOT move cabin, shrink paddle, spread or shift chimney or helm to make things artificially visible. Preserve the reference layout even where it obstructs the sightline. Modest smoke drifts to one side. Distant simple horizon, no HUD, no labels, no text. This is a camera evaluation image rather than glamorous concept shot.
