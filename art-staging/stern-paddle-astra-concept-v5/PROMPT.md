## Authoritative Stern Rule

The raised teal stern rail preserves the hull's normal plan footprint.

- In X/Y, the rail follows one continuous, smooth, outward-convex transom curve from port to starboard.
- At the center, that same curve rises only along Z to clear the paddle wheel, then returns to normal gunwale height.
- In orthographic top projection, the Z displacement collapses and the rail therefore appears as one uninterrupted hull-shaped stern arc.
- The rail must never turn inward around the wheel or form a U-shaped notch, horseshoe, or pair of fore-aft arms.

## Wheel Relationship

- The modular wheel cassette mounts to the back face of the rounded transom on the ship centerline.
- The wheel may project aft beyond the hull outline, but remains narrower than the beam.
- Axle hubs and dark socket rails connect below the elevated center span without changing the hull's plan silhouette.
- The wheel, axle, hubs, frame, and paddles remain interchangeable as one upgrade unit.

## 3D Modeling Note

Build the gunwale/transom curve in plan first. Raise its center control points vertically to create wheel clearance without moving them inward or outward in plan. This preserves the same stern footprint in every elevation and produces the correct bird's-eye silhouette.
