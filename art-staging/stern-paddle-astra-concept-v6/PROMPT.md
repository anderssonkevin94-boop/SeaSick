# Stern Paddle Astra Concept V6

Generated with the built-in image generation tool as a precise-object edit of V5, using the user's rough Photoshop top view as the authoritative layout.

## Authoritative Stern Geometry

- The curved port and starboard hull sides terminate at a broad, nearly flat transverse transom.
- A single teal rail spans directly from one transom corner to the other.
- In plan view, this rail is straight or only subtly bowed and remains perpendicular to the ship centerline.
- In elevation, the center of the same rail rises along Z to clear the paddle wheel and then returns to gunwale height.
- The vertical rise does not change the rail's X/Y coordinates.

## Wheel Module

- The complete paddle-wheel cassette mounts centrally to the outside/aft face of the transom.
- Wheel, paddles, axle, hubs, and frame sit behind the deck rather than on it.
- The hull and deck stop cleanly at the transom.
- Compact dark-steel attachment points connect the removable cassette without cutting a notch into the hull.

## 3D Modeling Rule

Build the hull with a broad clipped stern plane. Create the teal transom rail across that plane, then raise only its central vertices in Z for wheel clearance. Mount the modular wheel assembly aft of the plane on the centerline. Do not curve the rail around the wheel in plan and do not create a U-shaped stern opening.
