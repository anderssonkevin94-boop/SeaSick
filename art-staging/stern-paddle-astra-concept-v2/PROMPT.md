# Stern Paddle Astra Concept V2

Generated with the built-in image generation tool as a precise-object edit of the V1 concept sheet.

## Revision brief

Redesign only the stern paddle-wheel housing and immediate stern hull architecture. Remove the simple half-barrel cover and make the enclosure look purpose-built as an integral continuation of the hull, as if the ship were designed around its propulsion system from the keel up.

The teal gunwales sweep inward and down into substantial wooden shoulder structures around a recessed, horseshoe-like wheel aperture. The housing uses a shallow faceted crown with a center ridge, flared eaves, layered teal trim, dark-steel compression ribs, exposed axle hubs, large bolts, and triangular wooden gussets. The lower wheel remains open to the water and mechanically legible.

The silhouette subtly suggests an armored tail or protective shell without literal creature ornament. Broad planes, clean facets, restrained texture, and strong material separation keep the design compatible with SeaSick's toon-shader language.

## Invariants

- Preserve the ship proportions, hull, open deck, six cannons, crew, helm, and single chimney.
- Preserve the warm orange-red wood, muted teal trim, charcoal metal, and concept-sheet layout.
- Keep one stern-center paddle wheel with visible mechanical clearance.
- No cabin, sails, extra mast, cargo clutter, labels, or watermark.
- Avoid half-barrel, covered-wagon, or detached-canopy forms.
