# Compact player endgame ship — 24-gun proposal

Measured 34 m hull and 9.5 m beam, two gun decks with six guns per side on each deck (24 total), two masts, six square sails and one small jib. Crew reference artwork is 1.7 m tall. Keeps the established teal, timber, vermilion, ivory and brass art direction.

The companion image compares the proposal on the left to the brig on the right, normalized to 26 m by 7.8 m under a shared orthographic camera. Hull lengths exclude bowsprits.

This is an exterior visual proposal, not a production or gameplay integration. Gunports are presentation recesses, and interior decks, collisions, buoyancy and rigging animation still need production work. Existing gameplay progression and the approved brig are unchanged.
