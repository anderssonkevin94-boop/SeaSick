# Adventure brig — integrated stern cabin

Standalone Blender revision, not imported into Unity.

The inset rectangular deckhouse is replaced by full-width upper hull walls. Each side reuses the original hull's sheer-edge vertices; the rear wall extends the transom mesh. The forward bulkhead reaches the curved deck, and the quarterdeck follows the new upper wall contour. Windows are reseated onto the sides; stairs, rails and aft lanterns follow the widened structure.

The builder checks that all 22 cabin/hull seam edges are shared by two faces in their respective side meshes. Front, back and side renders are reviewed separately. This checks the specific structural join, not complete production topology or buoyancy.

Source: `tools/blender/adventure_brig_study_v3.py`. Model: `tools/blender/source/adventure-brig-design-v3.blend`. Previous studies remain intact.
