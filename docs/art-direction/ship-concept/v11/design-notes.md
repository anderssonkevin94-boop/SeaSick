# V11 — final craft and sail pass

Retains V10 colors and V9 smaller jib. Square sails have fuller bellies, restrained tension ripples, broad canvas panel variation, reinforced edge bindings and yard lashings. Jib has head halyard, tack tie, stay hanks and paired sheets.

Timber has restrained plank-group variation, darker exposed post end grain, worn stair noses and fine scuffs along the deck walkway. A fitted timber stern nameboard reads SEASICK and carries two small wave flourishes. Paint, canvas, iron and brass now have distinct roughness/metallic values.

Source: `tools/blender/adventure_brig_study_v11.py`. Model: `tools/blender/source/adventure-brig-design-v11.blend`. Standalone Blender review; no Unity export. New rigging is visual geometry, not a sailing simulation.
