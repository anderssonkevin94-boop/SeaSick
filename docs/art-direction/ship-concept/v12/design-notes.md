# V12 — cartoon cannon character

All twelve guns use a rounded heavy breech, a pinched barrel neck, an oversized flared brass muzzle and a recessed dark bore. Six deck guns have chunky red carriage cheeks, iron-rimmed wooden wheels, large brass hubs and visible trunnions. The six lower guns use a compact version of the same barrel silhouette sized for the existing ports.

Deck barrels and their supports were raised to clear the cap rail after the muzzle enlargement. Cannon count and station layout remain three guns per side at each level. V11 ship design and sails are retained.

Source: `tools/blender/adventure_brig_study_v12.py`. Model: `tools/blender/source/adventure-brig-design-v12.blend`. Standalone art review; no Unity export or gameplay changes.

Validation: six deck guns and six lower guns confirmed. Evaluated barrel meshes have zero triangle intersections with the deck cap rails, rail posts or post caps in the design pose. Close-up and full ship renders reviewed.
