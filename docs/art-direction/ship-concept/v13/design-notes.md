# V13 — much smaller triangular jib

Jib width, height, belly and foot shaping reduced to 55% about its forward tack, giving approximately 30% of V12 canvas area before final hull shaping. Hems, seams and stay hanks follow the new triangle; halyard and sheets terminate at the resized corners.

Retains V12 cannon design and the existing ship palette. Source: `tools/blender/adventure_brig_study_v13.py`. Model: `tools/blender/source/adventure-brig-design-v13.blend`. Standalone Blender review, no Unity export.
