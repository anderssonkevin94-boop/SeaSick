# V6 — fair hull, framed stern and two battery levels

Standalone Blender revision only; Unity remains untouched.

- Replaced the local widening deformation with a continuous analytic beam profile.
- Rounded the transom using a gridded shell and coherent deformation of attached details.
- Added curved stern crossbeams, swept supporting timbers, brass fasteners and a wave crest.
- Three individual deck cannons per side, plus three lower cannons per side (12 total). Lower gunports are cut through the hull shell.
- Sail refinement follows the reference supplied on September 16 at 22:00: full plain canvas, lifted foot centres, subtle panel seams and a curved rather than faceted jib.
- Retains the integrated cabin/hull structure, centre hatch and stair access.
