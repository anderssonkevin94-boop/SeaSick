# Maximum ship — scale study

A standalone visual study of the progression ceiling: a measured 46 m hull, 13 m beam, three masts, twelve square sails plus a small jib, and 72 cannon positions (12 per side on each of three decks). Crew reference artwork is 1.7 m tall. This is maximum physical armament, not the fully manned gameplay limit.

The comparison brig is normalized to its game dimensions, 26 m by 7.8 m, and both ships share one orthographic camera. Lengths exclude projecting bowsprits. The largest ship appears on the left.

Uses the established palette and cannon style. This is an exterior scale mockup: gunports use dark presentation recesses, and the interior decks, rigging, collision, buoyancy and gameplay need production work. It does not replace the approved brig or any Unity asset.
